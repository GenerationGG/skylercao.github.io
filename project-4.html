Turbojet & Turbofan Cycle Analysis

ENGRMAE 112 Propulsion — Prof. Liu · Skyler Cao · November 7, 2026

Download Full Report

Abstract

This report presents an on-design cycle analysis of a single-spool turbojet and a bypass turbofan engine with non-ideal components. A MATLAB program was written to step through the engine stations: diffuser, compressor, burner, turbine, and nozzle, plus the fan and fan nozzle for the turbofan. Using the component efficiencies and specific heat ratios from Phillip Hill's Thermodynamics and Mechanics of Propulsion, the program computes specific thrust, thrust specific fuel consumption (TSFC), and the thermal, propulsive, and overall efficiencies as functions of compressor pressure ratio for turbine inlet temperatures of 1500, 1600, and 1700 K at four flight conditions (sea level static, Mach 0.85, Mach 2, and Mach 3). At the same core airflow, a bypass ratio 10 turbofan produces about 3.3× the takeoff thrust of the turbojet, because it accelerates a much larger mass flow to a lower velocity.

1. Introduction

The objectives were to compare the takeoff thrust of a turbojet and a turbofan with the same core airflow, and to write a program carrying out the on-design analysis of each with real component efficiencies, reproducing the corresponding textbook figures. In a turbojet, all of the air passes through the compressor, burner, and turbine, and thrust comes from the high-velocity core jet. A turbofan adds a fan that accelerates an additional stream of air around the core; the ratio of bypass mass flow to core mass flow is the bypass ratio β.

1.2 Assumptions

  • Working fluid is a perfect gas with constant specific heat; γ varies between components per Tables 5.1/5.2. R = 287 J/(kg·K)
  • Component losses represented by adiabatic efficiencies; no total pressure loss in the burner (p04 = p03), burner efficiency η_b = 1
  • Mechanical shaft efficiency is 1 — turbine work equals compressor work (plus fan work for the turbofan)
  • Nozzles are fully expanded (exit static pressure equals ambient pressure)
  • Fuel–air ratio is small, but fuel mass is retained in turbine work balance and thrust equation

5. Key Results & Interpretation

  • Static turbojet: Specific thrust rises with compressor pressure ratio to a broad maximum (~1 kN·s/kg), then falls at very high pressure ratios as the turbine takes a larger share of available energy.
  • Supersonic cruise (M=2, M=3): The usable compressor pressure ratio range collapses rapidly with Mach number — roughly 60–100 at M=2, dropping to just 15–25 at M=3 — because ram temperature rise already does much of the compression work.
  • Turbofan thrust: Static specific thrust per unit core airflow is about 2× the turbojet's, with roughly half the fuel consumption — the direct bypass effect quantified in Problem 1.
  • Efficiencies: Thermal efficiency rises with pressure ratio before leveling off; propulsive efficiency initially falls then recovers at higher pressure ratios. The turbofan's propulsive efficiency is substantially higher than the turbojet's at every condition, since its mean jet velocity sits much closer to flight speed.

6. Conclusions

The program successfully reproduces the trends of all 10 referenced textbook figures (5.19–5.22, 5.29–5.34). Specific thrust has an optimum compressor pressure ratio at subsonic speed that shifts with T_max, and the useful pressure ratio range shrinks rapidly with flight Mach number. Raising T_max increases specific thrust and extends the usable pressure ratio range. Adding bypass flow raises thrust per unit core airflow and propulsive efficiency while lowering fuel consumption — for Problem 1, the β=10 turbofan delivers about 3.3× the turbojet's takeoff thrust at the same core airflow.

%% ======================== LOCAL FUNCTIONS ========================
function o = turbojet(Ma, P_rc, T04, Pa, Ta, E)
    R = E.R;
    cp_C = E.gamma_C*R/(E.gamma_C-1); cp_B = E.gamma_B*R/(E.gamma_B-1);
    cp_T = E.gamma_T*R/(E.gamma_T-1); cp_N = E.gamma_N*R/(E.gamma_N-1);
    V0 = Ma*sqrt(E.gamma_d*R*Ta);
    T02 = Ta*(1 + (E.gamma_d-1)/2*Ma.^2);
    P02 = Pa*(1 + E.eta_D*(E.gamma_d-1)/2*Ma.^2).^(E.gamma_d/(E.gamma_d-1));
    T03 = T02.*(1 + (P_rc.^((E.gamma_C-1)/E.gamma_C) - 1)/E.eta_C);
    P03 = P_rc.*P02;
    P04 = P03;
    f = (cp_B*T04 - cp_C*T03)./(E.eta_B*E.Q - cp_B*T04);
    T05 = T04 - cp_C*(T03 - T02)./((1+f)*cp_T);
    P05 = P04.*(1 - (1 - T05/T04)/E.eta_T).^(E.gamma_T/(E.gamma_T-1));
    T9 = T05.*(1 - E.eta_N*(1 - (Pa./P05).^((E.gamma_N-1)/E.gamma_N)));
    V9 = sqrt(2*cp_N.*(T05 - T9));
    Fs = (1+f).*V9 - V0;
    S = f./Fs;
    dKE = ((1+f).*V9.^2 - V0.^2)/2;
    etaT = dKE./(f*E.Q); etaP = Fs.*V0./dKE; etaO = etaT.*etaP;
    bad = imag(V9)~=0 | imag(P05)~=0 | imag(T9)~=0 | imag(Fs)~=0 | ...
          f<=0 | P05<=Pa | Fs<=0 | T05>=T04;
    o = pack(Fs,S,f,etaT,etaP,etaO,bad);
end

function o = turbofan(Ma, P_rc, P_rf, B, T04, Pa, Ta, E)
    R = E.R;
    cp_C = E.gamma_C*R/(E.gamma_C-1); cp_B = E.gamma_B*R/(E.gamma_B-1);
    cp_T = E.gamma_T*R/(E.gamma_T-1); cp_N = E.gamma_N*R/(E.gamma_N-1);
    cp_F = E.gamma_f*R/(E.gamma_f-1); cp_Fn = E.gamma_Fn*R/(E.gamma_Fn-1);
    V0 = Ma*sqrt(E.gamma_d*R*Ta);
    T02 = Ta*(1 + (E.gamma_d-1)/2*Ma.^2);
    P02 = Pa*(1 + E.eta_D*(E.gamma_d-1)/2*Ma.^2).^(E.gamma_d/(E.gamma_d-1));
    T013 = T02.*(1 + (P_rf.^((E.gamma_f-1)/E.gamma_f) - 1)/E.eta_F);
    P013 = P_rf.*P02;
    T03 = T02.*(1 + (P_rc.^((E.gamma_C-1)/E.gamma_C) - 1)/E.eta_C);
    P03 = P_rc.*P02;
    P04 = P03;
    f = (cp_B*T04 - cp_C*T03)./(E.eta_B*E.Q - cp_B*T04);
    T05 = T04 - (cp_C*(T03-T02) + B.*cp_F.*(T013-T02))./((1+f)*cp_T);
    P05 = P04.*(1 - (1 - T05/T04)/E.eta_T).^(E.gamma_T/(E.gamma_T-1));
    T9 = T05.*(1 - E.eta_N*(1 - (Pa./P05).^((E.gamma_N-1)/E.gamma_N)));
    V9 = sqrt(2*cp_N.*(T05 - T9));
    T19 = T013.*(1 - E.eta_Fn*(1 - (Pa./P013).^((E.gamma_Fn-1)/E.gamma_Fn)));
    V19 = sqrt(2*cp_Fn.*(T013 - T19));
    Fs = (1+f).*V9 + B.*V19 - (1+B)*V0;
    S = f./Fs;
    dKE = ((1+f).*V9.^2 + B.*V19.^2 - (1+B)*V0.^2)/2;
    etaT = dKE./(f*E.Q); etaP = Fs.*V0./dKE; etaO = etaT.*etaP;
    bad = imag(V9)~=0 | imag(P05)~=0 | imag(T9)~=0 | imag(Fs)~=0 | ...
          f<=0 | P05<=Pa | Fs<=0 | T05>=T04;
    o = pack(Fs,S,f,etaT,etaP,etaO,bad);
end

function o = pack(Fs,S,f,etaT,etaP,etaO,bad)
    f = f.*ones(size(Fs));
    bad = bad | isnan(Fs);
    Fs(bad)=NaN; S(bad)=NaN; f(bad)=NaN; etaT(bad)=NaN; etaP(bad)=NaN; etaO(bad)=NaN;
    o.Fs = real(Fs); o.S = real(S); o.f = real(f);
    o.etaT = real(etaT); o.etaP = real(etaP); o.etaO = real(etaO);
end
