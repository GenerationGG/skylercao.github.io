01 / ABOUT

What I Do

I'm an Aerospace Engineering student at UC Irvine (B.S., expected June 2027, GPA 3.4), focused on the intersection of propulsion, structures, and flight dynamics. As President of MAS Rocketry, I lead full-cycle development of custom solid propellant propulsion systems — from thermodynamic performance modeling to structural stress validation and avionics integration.

My work spans CFD/FEA validation in Ansys Fluent, CAD design in SolidWorks and Siemens NX, PCB/avionics development in Altium Designer, and orbital mechanics / trajectory optimization in MATLAB. I care about rigorous validation — every model I build gets checked against reference cases before it touches hardware.

Previously, as a Hardware Specialist with NASA's Community College Aerospace Scholars program, I designed a 12-wheeled lunar drilling rover capable of climbing 30° inclines while surviving a -330°C to 54°C thermal environment.

Interplanetary Trajectory Design & Mission Analysis

Modeled 3D transfer trajectories to NEO 65803 Didymos, finding a local-minimum ΔV of 8.5 km/s across a 1,000-day launch window.

MATLAB Lambert's Problem Orbital Mechanics Porkchop Plots

Architecture: Preliminary mission-analysis pipeline built in MATLAB that solves Lambert's problem across a dense grid of departure/arrival dates.

  • Exact spatial coordinate mapping of Earth (departure) and Didymos (arrival)
