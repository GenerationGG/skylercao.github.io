01 / ABOUT

What I Do

I'm an Aerospace Engineering student at UC Irvine (B.S., 2023–2028, GPA 3.4), focused on the intersection of propulsion, structures, and flight dynamics. As Club President / Propulsion Engineer of MAS Rocketry, I lead full-cycle development of custom solid propellant propulsion systems — from thermodynamic performance modeling to structural stress validation and avionics integration.

My work spans CFD/FEA validation in Ansys Fluent, CAD design in SolidWorks and Siemens NX, avionics/PCB development in Altium Designer, and orbital mechanics / astronautics / trajectory optimization in MATLAB & Simulink. I care about rigorous validation — every model I build gets checked against reference cases before it touches hardware.

Previously, as a Hardware Specialist with NASA's Community College Aerospace Scholars program, I collaborated within a multidisciplinary team of 12 engineers to design, prototype, and test a 12-wheeled lunar drilling rover capable of climbing 30° inclines while surviving a -330°C to 54°C thermal environment.
